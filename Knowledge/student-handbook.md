## Attendance Policy



Students must maintain at least 75% attendance to remain eligible for final examinations.



Late attendance may still count toward attendance according to institutional attendance rules.



## Fee Policy



Students are responsible for paying course fees according to the institutional fee schedule.



A student whose payment is incomplete may have a Partial payment status.



## Enrollment Policy



Students may enroll in available courses subject to institutional enrollment requirements.



An enrollment can later become Active, Completed, or Dropped according to the academic process.



## Exam Policy



Final examination eligibility may depend on institutional academic and attendance requirements.



Students should consult the applicable policy before assuming eligibility.
